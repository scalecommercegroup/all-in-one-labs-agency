## Design Context

### Users

Swedish growth companies with roughly 10–100 employees. Visitors are owners,
marketing leaders, ecommerce operators, and service managers looking for more
qualified demand or less manual customer handling. They arrive skeptical of
generic agencies and inflated AI claims, and need to understand the offer,
evidence, safeguards, and next step quickly.

### Brand Personality

Direct, operational, and assured. The brand should feel like an experienced
commercial team that happens to be technically strong—not a speculative AI
startup or a generic management consultancy.

### Aesthetic Direction

An editorial technology studio built from the existing AIOECOM identity:
deep navy, soft off-white, oversized grotesk typography, a globe-derived mark,
asymmetric layouts, and deliberate motion. The interface is light-first for
daytime business research, with dark sections used to demonstrate operational
systems. Avoid neon AI gradients, glass panels, decorative dashboards, and
repetitive card grids.

### Design Principles

1. Make the commercial outcome clear before explaining the technology.
2. Let typography, whitespace, and evidence create authority.
3. Show AI workflows as understandable sequences with human handoffs.
4. Use motion sparingly to explain relationships, never to delay reading.
5. Publish only claims that have an evidence record and approved placement.
